The Art of Making

Where Precision
Meets Passion

Every Lumière piece passes through four stages of creation — each one a discipline in its own right. From first pencil stroke to final polish, nothing is left to chance.

01

Design

Every creation begins as a hand-drawn sketch in our Parisian atelier. Our designers work in pencil and ink until the form feels inevitable.

02

Selection

Our gemologist travels globally to source only the finest stones. Fewer than 1 in 100 evaluated diamonds makes it into a Lumière creation.

03

Setting

Our master setters — each with over 20 years of practice — hand-place every stone with jeweler's precision. The setting is as considered as the stone itself.

04

Finishing

A 48-hour final polish and quality review under daylight-spectrum lighting. Only then does the piece receive our hallmark and leave the atelier.

Our Story

Born from a belief that beauty outlasts time

Founded in Paris in 1987, Lumière began as a single atelier on the Rue de la Paix. Our founder, Élise Moreau, believed that a jewel should carry more than beauty — it should carry meaning.

Thirty-seven years on, that philosophy guides every piece we create. From engagement rings to anniversary gifts, Lumière has been present for life's most luminous moments.
